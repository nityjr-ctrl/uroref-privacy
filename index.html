Overview

UroRef is a clinical reference app designed for urology trainees and healthcare professionals. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data UroRef collects, how it is used, and your rights.

Data We Collect

UroRef does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device.

Specifically:

We do not collect names, email addresses, or contact information. We do not collect location data. We do not collect usage analytics or tracking data. We do not use cookies or third-party trackers. We do not collect health or medical data from users.

On-Device Storage

UroRef uses your device's local storage (localStorage) solely to save your preferences (such as dark mode setting) and any stent tracker entries you create. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Third-Party Services

UroRef does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No data is shared with third parties.

Children's Privacy

UroRef is a professional medical reference tool and is not directed at children under 17. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version here with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: nityje@gmail.coma>

Medical Disclaimer

UroRef is a reference tool for qualified healthcare professionals. It is not a medical device, does not provide diagnoses, and should not replace clinical judgement. Always follow your local guidelines and trust protocols.
