# Acknowledgements & Attributions

This repository hosts the standalone privacy policy page for **UroRef** — a urology quick-reference app for trainees.

The privacy policy text itself is original, written for UroRef by Nity G (Urology SpR). Nothing in this repo is content that belongs to someone else.

---

## Styling & Fonts

The page uses the standard system-font stack (`-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif`). No third-party webfonts, CSS frameworks or JavaScript libraries are loaded. No external assets are bundled.

---

## About the app this policy applies to

UroRef is built on open-source software (React, Capacitor, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui patterns, Lucide icons, and others) and references published clinical guidelines (EAU, NICE, BAUS, AJCC/UICC, BNF) and peer-reviewed calculators (BURST IDENTIFY, BURST MIMIC, TWIST, CKD-EPI, CPG, EORTC, Clavien-Dindo).
